Request Account Deletion

Applies to: Agenzo – Smart Reminders (Android)

Option A — Delete inside the app (fastest)

If you still have access to the app:

  • Open Agenzo → Settings → Account → Delete account
  • This deletes your account and associated data stored in our Firebase backend.

Option B — Request deletion from the web (no app needed)

If you can’t access the app anymore, you can request deletion here:

🧾 Open Deletion Request Form

Important verification step: To prevent abuse, we will only process deletion after we confirm you control the account email. We may email you asking you to confirm your request.

What we delete

  • Your Agenzo account (Firebase Authentication user)
  • Your reminders, tasks, and location reminders stored in our database
