Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 Nov 2025

1. Who we are

Agenzo – Smart Reminders (“Agenzo”, “we”, “the App”) helps you create and receive reminders. This Policy explains what data we process, why, and how you can exercise your rights.

2. Data we process

  • Account data (optional): if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and profile photo to create your account.
  • Reminder data: titles, notes, due dates/times, and (if you enable it) location for geofenced reminders.
  • Device & diagnostics: device model, OS version, app version, crash logs and basic analytics to keep the App stable and improve features.
  • Notifications: we store the schedule needed to deliver push notifications on time.

3. Why we use your data

  • Provide the service (create, sync and deliver reminders and notifications).
  • Maintain safety, performance and reliability (diagnostics, crash reports, abuse prevention).
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable.

4. Where data is stored

  • On device: your reminders may be stored locally on your phone.
  • Cloud (optional): if you sign in, data may sync via Google Firebase services (e.g., Authentication, Firestore/Realtime Database, Analytics, Crashlytics). Actual services depend on features you use.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with service providers solely to operate the App (e.g., Google Firebase). Providers process data under our instructions and applicable terms.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications: to deliver reminders.
  • Location (optional): only if you enable location-based reminders. You can disable it at any time in system settings.

7. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can delete reminders in the App and, if signed in, request account deletion.

8. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may access, correct, delete, or export your data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age required by local law (e.g., 13/16). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Contact

Email: support@agenzo.app

11. Changes

If we change this Policy, we will update the “Last updated” date and publish the new version here.
