1) Scope

This Privacy Policy explains what data Agenzo (“we”, “us”) collects, how we use and share it, your choices, and your rights under applicable law (e.g., GDPR). By using the app, you agree to this policy. If you disagree, please stop using the service and contact us.

2) Data we collect and why

Account & authentication

What: Email address and authentication tokens when you sign in (Firebase Authentication via email/password or Google Sign-In).
Why: Secure your account, enable sign-in/out and password resets, and prevent unauthorized access.

Reminders & tasks (saved to your account)

What: Titles, notes, due date/time, time zone, completion status, and local notification IDs.
Why: Show your agenda across devices and schedule/cancel notifications.
Note: Voice transcripts returned by our server are copied into reminder notes so you can read/edit them later.

Location reminders (opt-in)

What: Latitude/longitude, radius, trigger mode (enter/exit), enable/disable flags, label, and last-triggered timestamp.
Why: Arm geofences locally and avoid duplicate triggers.

Device data & local caches (on your device)

What: Selected locale, consent/“don’t ask again” toggles, notification permission state, cached scheduled-notification data, deep-link payloads, and a pending-action queue (reminder ID, action such as Done/Snooze, and timestamp) for offline use.
Why: Avoid re-asking for permissions, reopen the right content from notifications, and replay actions when you come back online.

Voice input (opt-in)

What: Microphone audio recorded to a temporary file, your user ID, locale, and time zone.
Why: Convert speech to reminders/tasks (see §5 for processors).

Support & feedback

What (automatically attached): Your signed-in UID, account email, locale, time zone (IANA & offset), platform, OS version, device hostname, app version (if available), and timestamp, plus the message you type.
Why: Diagnose issues and respond effectively.

We do not collect: third-party analytics, advertising IDs, or marketing SDK data; continuous background location trails (geofencing runs on-device).

3) How we process data

  • Core storage: Reminders, tasks, and location reminders are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your user ID.
  • Notifications: We schedule local notifications (no marketing pushes). On Android, we may request exact alarms and register boot receivers so reminders persist after restarts.
  • Geofencing: Runs locally on your device; only trigger timestamps are written back.
  • Voice pipeline: Audio is uploaded securely, transcribed, optionally summarized/extracted, and the recognized transcript is added to your reminder notes. The temporary audio file on your device is deleted after processing.

4) Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contractual necessity: Provide reminders, tasks, notifications, and related features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: Service reliability, security, and fraud prevention (e.g., avoiding duplicate triggers, ensuring delivery accuracy).
  • Consent: Microphone, precise/background location, and notifications are optional OS-level permissions you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legal obligation: We may process data to comply with applicable law.

5) Third-party processors (who helps us operate the app)

  1. Google Firebase (Google LLC) — Authentication and Cloud Firestore storage for your reminders, tasks, and location records.
  2. Google Cloud Speech-to-Text — Processes audio clips you submit via Voice input to produce transcripts.
  3. Groq (Llama API) — Receives the transcript and your time-zone context (not the raw audio) to extract reminder/task details from speech.
  4. Google Maps Platform / Places API — Map rendering, autocomplete, and geocoding for location reminders. Requests may include your search text and coarse coordinates.
  5. SendGrid (Twilio SendGrid) — Transmits support/feedback emails; stores typical email metadata and message content for delivery/diagnostics.
  6. Platform plugins (Geolocator, geofencing/foreground-service frameworks, local notifications) — Operate on-device; they do not transmit your data to their publishers.

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with these processors or where required by law.

6) Data retention

  • Reminders, tasks, location reminders: Kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Completed items: Kept for history unless you delete them.
  • Voice audio files (on device): Deleted immediately after the server responds.
  • Voice transcripts: Persist in your reminder notes until you edit or delete them.
  • Device caches & pending-action queue: Reside only on your device; removed when you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Support/feedback: Stored in Firestore and sent via SendGrid; retained to handle and audit your request. Not automatically removed when you delete your account—email us to request deletion of support records.
  • System/operational logs (providers): Retained by processors per their policies for security/diagnostics.

7) International transfers

Data handled by Google, Groq, and SendGrid may be processed in the EU and/or other countries (including the US). These providers support recognized transfer mechanisms (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses). We apply the protections described here regardless of processing location.

8) Your rights

Subject to law, you may access, rectify, delete, restrict, object, or port your data.

  • In-app deletion: Deleting your account removes your reminders, tasks, and location reminders from Firestore immediately.
    Not automatically deleted: support/feedback submissions and email copies (SendGrid/outbox), and provider logs. Contact us to request their deletion; we’ll comply unless we must retain them (e.g., to resolve disputes or meet legal obligations).
  • Permissions: Revoke location/microphone/notifications in system settings at any time (features may stop working).
  • Other requests (access/export, etc.): Email sharpcreations4@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity.

9) Security

  • Account security via Firebase Authentication; per-user access control via Firestore security rules.
  • All app-server communications use HTTPS/TLS.
  • On-device data lives in the platform sandbox; temporary voice files are kept only in protected app directories.
  • Access to production data is limited to essential operations. Keep your OS updated and protect your device.

10) Permissions we request (Android/iOS)

  • Location (approximate/precise; “Allow always” may be requested)
  • Activity Recognition (Android)
  • Microphone
  • Notifications / Exact alarms (Android)
  • Foreground service (Android)

You can grant or revoke these permissions in system settings.

11) Analytics and crash reporting

We do not embed third-party analytics or Crashlytics. If distributed via app stores, we may receive aggregated stability metrics (e.g., crash/ANR counts) from store consoles. If we later add optional analytics/crash reporting, we will update this policy and ask for consent first.

12) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect product or legal changes. We’ll revise the Effective date above and, for material updates, notify you in-app or by email. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
